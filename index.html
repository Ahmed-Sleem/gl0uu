Questions, answered.

What does gl0uu analyse?

Using its lights and AI, gl0uu looks at your skin from your scan and shows the results in the app. The full list of measures will be shared before launch.

Is it a medical device?

No. gl0uu gives skin insights for general wellness and information. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. For medical concerns, please see a qualified professional.

Will it fit my phone?

gl0uu is designed for a wide range of phones with a universal clip. Tested compatibility will be published before shipping.

How is my data handled?

Your scans are saved in the app. Full privacy details will be published before launch.
